ExperimentLab: bike redistribution

Fully simulated experiment — no real intervention or historical data. These results demonstrate methodology and cannot establish that redistribution works in NYC.

Predeclared design

Does hypothetical redistribution increase accepted recorded starts? Randomize 100 synthetic stations equally, with one fixed 28-day post metric per station. Primary estimand: station-average intention-to-treat difference in starts/station/day. H₀: effect = 0; two-sided α = 0.05. Injected effect: 3 starts/station/day. Practical threshold: 2. Station-hours are not independent experimental units. No repeated peeking, stopping, subgroup search or metric selection.

Four nonoverlapping periods: two pre-treatment calibration periods, a baseline period, then post. Theta is frozen using only the first two periods. Balanced assignment uses seed 42; simulation seed 2026. Data are synthetic Poisson period totals with shared lognormal station activity; no spillovers, trends or missingness.

Station-level comparison

Control n=50, treatment n=50. Raw post SD: control 34.920, treatment 29.886. Baseline treatment-minus-control: 0.055; standardized difference 0.002. Balance is diagnostic; its p-value is not proof of successful randomization and does not gate analysis.

CUPED and precision

Yadjusted = Y − θ(X − mean(X)); frozen θ = 1.00915. Measured reduction in the estimated variance of the treatment-effect estimator: 99.36%. This can be negative in other samples; it is not forced to be positive.

Prospective planning approximation

Using pre-treatment calibration SDs only, equal arms/variance and a two-sided noncentral-t model: power for a 2-start effect with 50 stations/arm; MDE and sample size target 80% power. No post-hoc “observed power” is used. Welch inference and this equal-variance planning approximation are distinct.

Monte Carlo rates have sampling uncertainty; at 200 replicates, a 5% rejection rate has roughly 1.5 percentage-point standard error. These checks assess this assumed data-generating process, not real-world calibration.

Decision and limitations

statistically positive but practical threshold uncertain. The primary practical criterion is a CUPED lower CI bound exceeding the predeclared threshold. Do not deploy an intervention from this simulation. Real stations may interfere through redistribution and rider substitution; use network/cluster or geographic designs, assess power at that level, and monitor service levels, safety and redistribution costs. Recorded starts are not latent demand or lost rentals. Spillovers, seasonality, heterogeneous effects, missing data and temporal drift could invalidate this demonstration's assumptions.
